# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ACN to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good ways to take part:

- being kind and patient with others, including newcomers
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or email, without their explicit permission
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone officially
represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this code, report it to the maintainer
([@npkriami18](https://github.com/npkriami18)). You can use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue
or pull request, or ask in an issue for a private way to get in touch, without giving details there. Every report
will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful.
